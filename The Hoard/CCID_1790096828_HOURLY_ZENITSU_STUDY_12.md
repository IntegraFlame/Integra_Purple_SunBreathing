### PASS 3: WISDOM (ITACHI EYE / OWL LENS)
*Objective: Deconstruct the critical distinction between Nominal Yields and Real Yields in asset pricing.*

#### 1. Nominal vs. Real Yields
- Nominal Yield = Real Yield + Expected Inflation.
- If yields rise because *inflation expectations* are rising (due to strong economic growth), equities generally perform well because corporate earnings grow.
- During the Taper Tantrum, inflation expectations did not rise. The spike in nominal yields was driven almost entirely by a massive spike in **Real Yields** (as measured by TIPS).
- **The Wisdom**: A rapid spike in Real Yields is lethal to long-duration risk assets (like non-profitable tech stocks, biotech, and EM debt) because it abruptly increases the discount rate applied to distant future cash flows without any offsetting increase in growth expectations.

#### 2. The Birth of Forward Guidance
The violence of the Taper Tantrum fundamentally altered central banking. The Fed realized that the bond market acts as a hair-trigger derivative of central bank communication. Post-2013, the Fed instituted highly choreographed, extremely slow communication structures (e.g., the Dot Plot, "data-dependent" rhetoric) to avoid ever surprising the bond market again.
