- **LESSON**: In low-vol, SELL premium. Don't buy it. Cheap ≠ profitable.

**Pattern 2: Debit Spreads (BUY_CALL_SPREAD, BUY_PUT_SPREAD) Have 0% Win Rate**
- BUY_CALL_SPREAD: 6 uses → 0 wins → -$1,693.91
- BUY_PUT_SPREAD: 3 uses → 0 wins → -$1,433.08
- **LESSON**: Debit spreads require not just direction, but MAGNITUDE. The spread caps your upside but doesn't protect from theta bleeding your premium if the move is too slow.

**Pattern 3: Selling Puts (CSP) in Low Vol = Small but Consistent**
- SELL_PUT_CSP: 6 uses → 6 wins → +$92.53
- 100% win rate, but tiny returns (+0.1% avg). The premium collected in low vol is minuscule.
- **LESSON**: CSPs work reliably, but need to be scaled up or combined with other legs.

**Pattern 4: Crisis Quarters Generate Outsized Returns**
- 2002 Q3 (BUY_PUT_PROTECTIVE, VIX 35): +$1,031.98 (+10.3%)

**Pattern 6: PREMIUM_HARVEST (/MES Put Spreads) is the Highest-Consistency Strategy**
- 15 uses → 100% win rate → +$1,900.53 total (+1.3% avg)
- NEVER LOST. Every single time the engine sold low-delta put spreads on /MES in low-vol, it won.
- **LESSON**: This IS the Friday Fortress signature move. Low-delta (0.08-0.10) put credit spreads on /MES during VIX < 15 have an empirical 100% success rate across 15 quarters spanning 6 years.

**Pattern 7: Crisis Detection = Outsized Alpha**
- 2008 Q4 (CRISIS_PUT_BUYING, VIX 56): +$715.99
- 2009 Q1 (CRISIS_PUT_BUYING, VIX 45): +$271.65
- Combined +$987.64 from just 2 quarters
- **LESSON**: When VIX > 35, the system MUST buy puts. The payout asymmetry is massive.

**Pattern 8: The Bear Put Spread False Positive Trap**

- **LESSON**: After a crisis, bearish trend signals PERSIST even as the market recovers. Need a VIX *direction* filter, not just VIX *level*.
