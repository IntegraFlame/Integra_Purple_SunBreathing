## What it says (isolated read)
- Metacognition = planning, monitoring, evaluating and changing one's own learning. Two dimensions: **knowledge** (of one's abilities, of the task, of strategies and when to use them) and **regulation** (monitoring and control).
- **Nelson & Narens (1990):** two levels. The **object level** does the work (e.g. decoding text). The **meta level** watches it: **monitoring** carries information up (did I understand that paragraph?) and **control** sends changes down (re-read, slow down, use a dictionary).
- **Perkins (1992), four kinds of learner:** tacit (unaware), aware (knows some of its thinking, not deliberate), strategic (organises thinking with chosen strategies), reflective (monitors whether a strategy is working *while* using it and switches).
- Related terms: self-regulated learning (adds motivation); metamemory.
- **Evidence:** metacognition predicts learning over and above cognitive ability and can compensate for cognitive limits; it helps transfer between tasks and domains.
- **Misconceptions:** it need not be deliberate (habitual error-checking counts); it is not only for older learners (error correction at 18 months).
- **Practice:** explicit goals with self-assessment against them; progression from explicit instruction to independent practice and structured reflection; scaffolds that fade; teachers think aloud; reciprocal teaching's four strategies (**summarise, question, clarify, predict**); "exam wrappers" (reflect before and after feedback); embed strategies in subject teaching, not as generic drills; evaluate the impact of each new strategy.

## Links to the earlier documents (compounding)
- **Nelson–Narens is the cleanest frame for Integra's monitors.** Object level = the working models (Y789/Nexus, Rodin's retrieval); meta level = Heimdall (monitoring) and Cheshire/Heimdall gates (control). Monitoring flows up as signals; control flows down as actions. The meta level does not do the task itself. Document 06's "fast monitor + slow monitor" sits entirely in the meta level.
- **Perkins' ladder as a maturity scale for each lobe:** tacit (no signal), aware (logs only), strategic (chooses among strategies), reflective (measures the strategy's success in flight and switches). Most Integra lobes measured so far are tacit or aware (constants reported as status); the goal is reflective, proven by measurements.
- **Exam wrappers = pre-registered predictions:** before an SWDS cycle or a task, write what you expect; afterwards, compare. That gives document 06's calibration data for free, and it is the shape of the TaskNN reports ("what is true now / what is not").
