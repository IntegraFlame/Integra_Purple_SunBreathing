## What it says (isolated read)
- A new class of traveling waves **rotates** over space and time, centred mostly on the somatosensory cortex, so it **sweeps in sequence across the body map**.
- The local wiring matches: axons in that region point around a circle ("merry-go-round"); a computational model shows this architecture supports rotation; **cutting the local circuit reduced rotating waves in motor cortex** (causal evidence).
- Waves are **mirrored** between hemispheres and between sensory and motor areas, following the long-range connections; thalamus, striatum and midbrain neurons track them moment to moment.
- Waves are modulated by arousal, evoked by a whisker air puff (clockwise sequences), and **selectively recruited during correct performance** of a visual-motor task.
- Interpretation (hypothesis): a space-and-time clock that orders sensation → action, helps predict sensory sequences, and may entrench practised skills. Direction and timing of propagation are dictated by the **geometry of the connections**. Human generality is unknown.
